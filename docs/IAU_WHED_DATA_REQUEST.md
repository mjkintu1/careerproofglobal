**Subject:** Data licence enquiry: World Higher Education Database (WHED) for a global career guidance platform

Dear WHED team,

I am **[FILL IN: name]**, founder of **CareerProof Global** (https://careerproofglobal.vercel.app), a career guidance platform that helps students and young professionals understand careers, the qualifications that lead to them, and the institutions that offer those qualifications. We started in Uganda and are building for students in every country.

WHED is the authority on recognised higher education institutions and credentials worldwide, and we would like to use it properly. Our site respects your robots.txt and AI-crawler settings, so we have **not** collected any data from whed.net; we would like to agree a licence instead.

**What we would like to use**
- Institution records: name, country, city, website, institution type, and recognition or accreditation status.
- Credentials and fields of study offered by each institution.
- Country education-system descriptions and credential frameworks.

**How we would use it**
- To show students whether an institution is recognised before they apply, alongside the career pages that link qualifications to occupations (ISCO-08, ESCO).
- With clear attribution to IAU/WHED and a link to the WHED record on every page that shows WHED data.
- Refreshed on your release schedule; we would remove or correct records promptly at your request.

**What we can offer**
- Attribution and referral traffic to WHED.
- Reports of errors or changes that our users and partner institutions flag, sent back to you.
- **[FILL IN: any partnership offer, e.g. joint outreach in East Africa, research collaboration, or IAU membership/affiliation if relevant]**

**Questions**
1. Is WHED data available under a licence for a platform like ours, and on what terms (attribution, fees, commercial or non-commercial use)?
2. Is there an export or API, and how often is it updated?
3. Are there conditions on how recognition status must be presented to students?

### Notes for the founder (do not send)
